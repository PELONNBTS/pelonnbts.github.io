✦   Aftercare & Healing

The surface of a tattoo typically heals in 2–4 weeks. The deeper layers of skin take up to 3–6 months to fully heal. During the surface healing phase follow your aftercare instructions carefully. Check out our full Aftercare Guide for detailed instructions.

Completely normal. Peeling usually starts around days 4–7 and can last another week. Do NOT pick at peeling skin let it fall off naturally. Picking pulls ink out and can cause scarring and uneven healing. Keep it moisturized and be patient.

No this is completely normal and called the "milky phase." A layer of healing skin forms over the tattoo making it look dull or faded. Once that layer sheds the colors and contrast come back. Give it the full 4–6 weeks before judging how it looks.

We recommend avoiding heavy workouts for at least one week excessive sweating can irritate and infect a fresh tattoo. Light activity is okay but avoid anything that causes heavy sweating directly on the tattooed area or stretches the skin significantly.
