We Stand Behind Our Work
Touch-Up Policy

We take pride in every piece that leaves this studio. Here is our clear, honest policy on touch-ups so there are no surprises.

Our Promise

We stand behind our work. If a tattoo heals unevenly due to normal variation in skin or an issue with our application, we will make it right at no charge. We are a family studio — your satisfaction matters to us personally.

The Policy
How Touch-Ups Work
⏰
Wait Time Required

Your tattoo must be fully healed before a touch-up — minimum 6 weeks, ideally 8–12 weeks. The tattoo needs to complete the full healing cycle before we can assess what needs to be corrected. Do not contact us about touch-ups before 6 weeks.

✅
Complimentary Touch-Ups

We offer one free touch-up session within 6 months of your original appointment if the tattoo healed with issues that were caused by our application — such as patchy shading, ink fallout in areas where aftercare was followed correctly, or lines that are uneven.

💰
Paid Touch-Ups

Touch-ups required due to improper aftercare, picking or peeling, sun damage, excessive fading from lifestyle factors, or changes in the client's skin are charged at our standard hourly rate. Touch-ups on work done by other studios are treated as new tattoos.

📍
Placement Exceptions

Certain placements are known to fade or require touch-ups by nature — hands, fingers, feet, inner lip, and heavily flexed areas. Fading in these areas is expected and touch-ups are not complimentary. This is discussed during consultation so there are no surprises.

🎨
Color Tattoos

Certain colors — especially white, light pastels, and some reds — may require a touch-up after full healing as they can appear different on different skin tones. One touch-up for color settling is included at no charge within 6 months when aftercare has been followed.

🦁
Apprentice Work

All work by Eric, Mousie, and Roo is supervised and reviewed by Jorge "Pelon". Touch-up policy for apprentice work follows the same guidelines — one complimentary session within 6 months for application issues. Pelon may personally review and complete touch-ups on apprentice work.

Quick Reference
Covered vs Not Covered
✓   Covered (Free)
Patchy ink from application
Uneven lines from tattooing
Ink fallout during healing (with proper aftercare)
Color settling issues within 6 months
Normal skin healing variation
✗   Not Covered (Paid)
Picking or peeling the tattoo
Sun damage or tanning on fresh ink
Not following aftercare instructions
Fading from hands, feet, fingers by nature
Requests outside 6-month window
