Deposit Policy
Securing Your Appointment
💰

$50 Deposit Required

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. This goes toward the total cost of your tattoo.

🚫

Non-Refundable

Deposits are non-refundable. If you need to reschedule, give us at least 24 hours notice and your deposit will carry over.

✅

Applied to Your Tattoo

Your deposit is deducted from the final price of your tattoo at your appointment.

📞

No-Show Policy

No-call no-shows forfeit their deposit and will be required to pay a new deposit to rebook.

The Process
What to Expect
01
📅
Book Online

Select your artist, service, and preferred time through our Square booking system. Pay your $50 deposit to confirm.

02
💬
Consultation

We'll discuss your design, placement, size and any reference images. Large or complex pieces may require a separate free consultation.

03
✍️
Sign Your Waiver

Complete your digital tattoo consent waiver before your appointment. You can sign online at nbtstucson.com/waiver.html

04
🎨
Get Tattooed

Sit back, relax and trust the process. We create a clean, comfortable experience from start to finish.

05
🌿
Aftercare

We'll send you home with full aftercare instructions. Follow them carefully to ensure your tattoo heals beautifully.

06
🦁
You're Family

Once you sit in our chair you're not just a client you're family. We're here for every piece of your journey.

Before You Come In
Important Reminders
🪪
Must Be 18+Valid government-issued photo ID required. No exceptions.
🚫
No Alcohol or DrugsDo not come to your appointment under the influence. We will not tattoo you.
🍽️
Eat Before You ComeHave a good meal before your appointment. Low blood sugar makes for a tough session.
📸
Bring Reference PhotosIf you have a design in mind bring reference images the more detail the better.
👕
Wear Comfortable ClothingWear clothing that allows easy access to the area being tattooed.
☀️
No SunburnDo not come in with a sunburn on the area being tattooed. We'll need to reschedule.
💧
Stay HydratedDrink plenty of water in the days leading up to your appointment. Hydrated skin takes ink better.
✝️
Walk-ins WelcomeNo appointment? Come on in Mon–Sat 9am–10pm. We'll do our best to fit you in.
