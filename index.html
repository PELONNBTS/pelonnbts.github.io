Our Story
New Beginnings Tattoo Studio

More than a tattoo shop — New Beginnings is a family. Founded by master artist Jorge "Pelon" Rodriguez with over 30 years of experience, NBTS is rooted in faith, driven by family, and dedicated to producing fine art that lasts a lifetime.

Every piece tells a story. Every client walks out carrying a piece of who they are, who they were, and who they are becoming. That is what we do here.

Jorge "Pelon"
Founder & Master Artist · 30+ Years

I started tattooing at 13 years old right here in Tucson and I have never stopped. Over 30 years of perfecting black and grey realism, Chicano art, portrait tattoos and cover-ups. Every piece I put on a person is permanent — I treat it with the respect and the level of skill that a lifetime of dedication deserves.
