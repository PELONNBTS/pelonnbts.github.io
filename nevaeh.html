Hey, I'm Mousie! As a tattoo artist, my passion lies in turning bold concepts into striking, custom body art. My work is heavily influenced by gothic aesthetics, dark surrealism, cyber-sigilism, and classic Chicano-style art focusing on sharp contrast, intricate linework, and deep detail.

I pride myself on maintaining a clean, comfortable, and collaborative studio space where we can bring your vision to life. Whether you're looking for a custom piece or wanting to grab some flash, let's collaborate on your next tattoo!
