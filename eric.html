✦   01   ·   The Eldest   ✦

I'm a tattoo apprentice with a passion for black and grey realism, bold creativity, and pushing my art in every direction possible. I'm heavily inspired by realism work, but I also love experimenting with different styles and adding a fresh, new school twist to my designs. Whether it's detailed portraits, playful concepts, or custom pieces with personality, I'm always looking to create something unique and meaningful.

Outside of tattooing, I bring an outgoing, lighthearted energy into everything I do. I love connecting with people, making the experience comfortable and fun, and turning ideas into art that clients are excited to wear forever. As I continue growing in the industry, I'm focused on sharpening my craft, learning every day, and building a style that blends strong technique with creativity and individuality.
