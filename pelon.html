Pelon
New Beginnings Family Tattoo Studio · Tucson, AZ
"I tattoo what God delivered me from — and what He's calling me toward. Every piece is a testimony."

Pelon is the founder of New Beginnings Family Tattoo Studio and has been putting ink on skin for over 20 years. His art is rooted in the Chicano tradition — bold, expressive, deeply personal — but his range goes far beyond. From hyper-realistic black and grey to vibrant color work, from sacred imagery to dark fantasy, Pelon does it all with a master's hand and a servant's heart.

His faith drives everything. Every client who sits in his chair gets more than a tattoo — they get an artist who genuinely cares about their story, their vision, and the mark they'll carry for life. New Beginnings isn't just a name. It's a promise.
